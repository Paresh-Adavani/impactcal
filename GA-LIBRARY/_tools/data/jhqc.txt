PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Adoni Tech’s JHQ-C series mechanical buffers are engineered for high-performance
energy absorption in industrial environments. These buffers feature robust construction,
precision damping, and easy mounting for versatile applications.

Website: adonitech.co.in
Series: JHQ-C Industrial Mechanical Buffers
Application: Impact absorption and vibration damping for industrial machinery


Each buffer is mounted via a base plate with four holes. Refer to the technical
drawing for installation guidance. Custom configurations available upon request.


FEATURES
  High energy absorption with compact design

  Durable elastomeric core for long service life
  Easy installation with standard bolt patterns

  Suitable for conveyors, presses, and automated systems
