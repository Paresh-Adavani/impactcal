Questions dealers ask

Do I quote in my own company name?

Yes. Your quotations carry your letterhead, logo, GSTIN and bank details. ADONI TECH bills you directly at your dealer price.

Will ADONI TECH approach my customers?

No. Nothing on your quotation names us, and enquiries you register stay yours.

Do I need engineering knowledge?

No. The ImpactCal selectors do the calculation; our engineers are a call away for difficult applications.

Is there a joining fee or minimum order?

There is no fee to apply. Commercial terms are agreed with you personally at approval.

Can overseas companies apply?

Yes. We work with partners abroad; export quotations are in USD and terms are agreed per partner.
